01 / 10

Money moves fast, ownership doesn't

269 / 280

You can send money to a friend in seconds. But changing who owns a bond, a business loan or a slice of a building? That still runs on paperwork, middlemen and "allow 3-5 business days". Redbelly is an Australian network built to fix that. Here it is in plain English.

02 / 10

What Redbelly is

279 / 280

So what is Redbelly? A public digital network for keeping track of who owns what, built for real-world things like bonds, loans, property and carbon credits. Businesses, fund managers and investors all look at the same up-to-date record, instead of each keeping their own copy.

Sources S1S6S8
03 / 10

Where it came from

219 / 280

It wasn't dreamt up in a garage. Redbelly grew out of research at the University of Sydney, working with CSIRO, Australia's national science agency. Work started in 2016. The public network went live in November 2024.

Sources S2S8
04 / 10

Tokenisation, as a pizza

263 / 280

The big idea has a clunky name: "tokenisation". Plain version: picture a big asset (a building, a fund, a bundle of loans) as a pizza. Redbelly lets the owner cut it into digital slices that can be issued, held and traded, each one with a clear owner on record.

Sources S1S6S8
05 / 10

The rules travel with the slice

274 / 280

Here's what makes Redbelly different: the rules travel with the slice. Many investments are only legally open to certain buyers. On Redbelly, identity and eligibility are checked at the exact moment of the trade. Like a bouncer built into the door, not standing beside it.

Download PNG
Sources S3S8
06 / 10

One private, reusable ID

279 / 280

And you don't hand over your passport a hundred times. Users are verified with a reusable digital ID, so they skip repeating the same checks on every platform. Clever maths ("zero-knowledge proofs") lets you prove you passed the check without sharing all your personal details.

Sources S2S3S4
07 / 10

Done means done

196 / 280

When a trade is done, it's done. No "pending" for days. Once a transaction is confirmed on Redbelly, it's final and can't be reversed. Think handing over cash, not waiting for a cheque to clear.

Sources S3S4
08 / 10

Tested by Australia's central bank

279 / 280

Has anyone serious tested it? In Project Acacia, Australia's central bank (the RBA) tested digital money in these markets. Redbelly was one of only three networks used for the RBA's trial digital money. In one pilot, a company bond was bought with that money, both on Redbelly.

Sources S6S7
09 / 10

Three steps become one

277 / 280

Another pilot on Redbelly shows why it matters. Paying off a loan usually takes 3 steps in separate systems: payment, loan closed, then the collateral (what backs the loan) handed back. On Redbelly, all 3 happened in a single step, so the collateral was free again instantly.

Sources S6

Why it matters

276 / 280

Why should you care? This is the plumbing behind money and ownership. Redbelly in one line: real-world things split, owned and traded quickly, with ID checks and the rules built in. Tokeniser, a platform built on Redbelly, reports A$1.2B issued for 160+ companies and funds.
