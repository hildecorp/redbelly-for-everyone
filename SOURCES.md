## Notes on how the sources were used

- **The independent source wins.** For the Project Acacia claims, the thread uses only
  what the RBA's own final report (S6) says, not Redbelly's description of it.
- **Company figures are labelled.** The A$1.2B and 160+ numbers are Tokeniser's own
  reported figures, and the thread says "reports" so readers know who is making the claim.
- **Left out on purpose.** Throughput figures (transactions per second) are quoted
  differently on different Redbelly pages, so the thread does not use them. It also
  leaves out roadmap items and partnerships that are announced but not yet finished,
  plus anything to do with prices, tokens or investing.
- **Illustrations are labelled.** The pizza comparison, the bouncer comparison, the
  cash-versus-cheque comparison and the names on image 2 are illustrations, not facts
  about Redbelly. Image 2 carries the note "Made-up names, for illustration".
